___SUMMARY___

Team reviewed the non-LLM classifier ADR, established a ~4,000-token baseline, and got shared Claude API access set up. Agreed to split classifier work three ways and converge Friday.



Classifier ADR & Baseline

Archer hit 100% precision at 59% coverage (46 of 78 held-out files); rest fall through to LLM.

Baseline token estimate: ~4,000 tokens across 3 LLM calls on Astermind unclassified file list.

Business & Team Direction

New hire Tim Blunt handles sales/pricing; team likely becomes cybersecurity pod.
